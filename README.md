## About this entry

This entry takes the long way round on purpose. It opens with the full ceremony of lawmaking — six solemn hearings, strict rules drafted for artificial intelligence, blockchain, and the entire internet — so the authority feels real and heavy. Then the sentence turns: the same room cannot perform the smallest office task involved in publishing its own bill. The defeat is not abstract tech illiteracy but a specific, visual fumble — printing the document out, scanning it back in crooked, and asking a staffer whether a photograph of the page counts as a PDF. The joke aims at the gap between the power to regulate complex technology and the competence on display at the dais, and it saves that photograph question for the very last words so the punch lands at the end.
